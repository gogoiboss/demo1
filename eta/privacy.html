Privacy Policy & Telemetry Governance

RippleETA processes calibrated train ETA predictions, corridor signaling events, and network delay propagation. This document details how data is handled, stored, and protected across our operational stakeholder gateways.

✓
Zero Personal Tracking
We do not track passenger GPS location or personal movement history. All telemetry is centered strictly on train numbers and station milestones.
&lock;
Conformal Data Privacy
Prediction intervals (P10–P90) are derived using conformalized historical distributions with cryptographic SHA-256 provenance logging.
🛡
Encrypted Transmission
All API payload exchanges utilize TLS 1.3 protocol standards with role-based JWT session authorization.
SECTION 01

Data Collection & Information Governance

RippleETA collects telemetry necessary to execute calibrated delay prediction algorithms across Indian Railways corridors. Data sources fall into two primary categories:

  • Train Movement Telemetry: Station arrival/departure timestamps, actual vs. scheduled delay minutes, temporary speed restriction (TSR) flags, and track section occupancy states obtained from official railway data channels (e.g. RailRadar).
  • Operator Session Data: Authenticated role tokens (Station Master, Crew Controller, Feeder Operator, Control Room) and associated email identifiers passed via Google OAuth 2.0 or local demo sessions.
Important: Passengers using public ETA views do not require account creation, and no biometric or location tracking cookies are used.
SECTION 02

Telemetry Processing & Conformal Predictions

Telemetry data is passed through our multi-stage calibration pipeline:

  • Stage 1 (Feature Engineering): Calculates delay momentum, headway margins, and weather risk scores.
  • Stage 2 (Conformal Calibration): Computes distribution bounds (P10 lower bound, P50 median, P90 upper bound) to deliver empirical coverage without compromising individual train operational confidentiality.
  • Stage 3 (Audit Logging): Persists model execution state into prediction_audit_log with dataset SHA-256 hashes and git commit revisions for auditability.
SECTION 03

Session Security & Credentials

Session security is maintained through HTTP-only authorization headers and encrypted JWT tokens signed with HMAC-SHA256. Demo accounts operate within isolated session scopes and expire automatically.

We strictly enforce role-based access control (RBAC). Privileged actions (such as platform assignment triage by Station Masters or relief dispatch by Crew Controllers) require valid session validation.

SECTION 04

Data Retention & Compliance

Prediction audit records are retained for historical evaluation, model calibration audits, and performance verification. Personal identifier fields in session logs are automatically scrubbed upon session termination.

RippleETA complies with applicable Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) standards and Indian Railways operational data guidelines.
